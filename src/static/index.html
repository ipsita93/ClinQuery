Data & methodology


  
Public-safe demonstration


  

    All records in this application are synthetic and are intended
    solely to demonstrate clinical research informatics and healthcare
    data engineering workflows.
  


  

    The underlying model is inspired by OMOP conventions and includes
    person, visit, condition, drug, concept, and location data.
  


  

    OMOP concept names are used for demonstration and do not represent
    a licensed vocabulary extract.
